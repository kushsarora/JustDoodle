# UI Review Captures

Actual simulator screenshots from the automated workflows, with no image edits. They document layout and behavior; the short test strokes are not finished artwork for the store listing.

The final September 11 home redesign is shown in `iPhone-Home.png` and `iPhone-Home-LargeText.png`, both on iPhone SE (3rd generation). The latter requests the largest accessibility text setting; home controls cap their scaling at accessibility size 1 and keep all actions visible. `iPad-Home.png` shows the same design with the content grouped and centered on iPad Pro 13-inch.

September 9 captures: `iPhone-Result.png` and `iPhone-LargeText.png` show the unchanged result and drawing screens on iPhone SE (3rd generation).

`iPad-Landscape.png` captures the rotated canvas on iPad Pro 13-inch, including strokes made before and after rotation.

Capture final App Store images after creating real drawings and confirming the required device sizes in App Store Connect. More detailed failure/interaction attachments remain in the local xcresult bundles referenced by `../REVIEW.md`.
