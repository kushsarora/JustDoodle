## Description

A line. A little imagination. A drawing that's yours.

Just Doodle starts with a random scribble and gives you a pen. Build something from the line, keep the happy accidents, and see where it takes you.

CLASSIC
Three minutes, one black pen, and no erasing. Finish whenever you're ready, or let the timer call time.

CHALLENGES
Try Quick Spark, Build It, Mood Lines, or Soundtrack Sketch. Each has its own prompt, time limit, and ink palette. Bring your own music to Soundtrack Sketch.

YOUR OWN RULES
Set a 1, 3, 5, 10, or 15-minute challenge with one, two, or three ink colors.

IDEA BOX
Tap for a fresh one-word drawing idea whenever inspiration runs dry.

YOUR DOODLE BOOK
Keep finished drawings on your device. Resume an unfinished drawing after an interruption. Save to Photos or share through your favorite apps.

No accounts. No ads. No sound effects. Just a time-up vibration you can turn off.

Made for iPhone and iPad. Draw with your finger or Apple Pencil on supported iPads.

## Review Notes

No account or login is required. No purchases, ads, analytics, or server connection are present.

Tap the black dot on the home screen to start Classic. The scribble reveals, then a three-minute timer starts. Draw with a finger or Apple Pencil. Tap Done to finish early; the drawing is saved to the local Doodle Book. Use the Photos or share icons on the result.

Challenges are available from the home screen. The timer continues during interruptions; an expired recovered draft opens as a finished drawing.

The app requests add-only Photos access only after the user taps Save to Photos. Denying access does not block drawing, the Doodle Book, or sharing.

Settings includes the full privacy policy, public policy link when configured, support email, and a time-up vibration toggle.
