Just Doodle Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026.

Your drawings belong to you

Just Doodle does not collect or send your drawings, personal information, or usage data to us. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics, or tracking SDKs in the app.

Local storage and backups

Drawings, unfinished drafts, and preferences are stored on your device. They may be included in backups managed by Apple according to your device settings. Just Doodle does not operate a cloud storage service.

Photos and sharing

Photos access is requested only when you choose Save to Photos. The app can add the drawing you select; it does not read your photo library.

Sharing is optional. When you use the system share sheet, the drawing and caption are sent to the app or recipient you choose. That service's privacy policy applies. Just Doodle does not upload drawings automatically.

Deleting your data

Delete drawings in the Doodle Book, or discard an unfinished draft. Removing Just Doodle from your device removes its local data. Copies already exported, shared, or backed up must be managed in those locations.

Support and contact

Email kushsarora@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. Support emails are used to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information or private drawings unless you intend to share them with support.
