# Branding Generation Record

Created September 27, 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool, not the API/CLI fallback. Selected raster outputs are stored in the app asset catalog and `Branding/`; discarded variants are not shipped. The icon was mechanically resized from 1254 x 1254 to 1024 x 1024 for the asset catalog.

## App Icon

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final production iOS app icon, one square 1024x1024 PNG, not a presentation mockup.
Primary request: design a beautiful, distinctive, extremely clean hand-inked monogram for Just Doodle, a native doodling game. Its world is white notebook pages, slightly imperfect black marker linework, handwritten signatures, and small blue ink accents.
Subject: exact lowercase letters "jd." in custom bold black felt-tip handwriting, one expressive unified signature. The j must have its dot, the d a clear open counter, period small and deliberate. Friendly confident chunky strokes with gently organic edges, exceptionally readable at 40px. Contemporary independent drawing-club identity, not corporate and not childish.
Composition: centered large monogram occupying about 65 percent of square width, generous 16 percent clear margins. One short gently curved blue ink underline beneath the letters. White full-bleed opaque background #FFFFFF, black #151515, blue #205FA0. Only these elements.
Constraints: full square opaque artwork, no rounded app-icon mask, no outer border, no shadows, no 3D, no ruled lines, no paper grain, no gradients, no stars/sparkles, no pencil objects, no hands, no decorative confetti, no extra text. No thin cursive hairlines. Not a logo sheet. Deliver a single finished image.
```

## Transparent Monogram

```text
Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent app brand mark for Just Doodle.
Input image is the approved "jd." monogram app icon. Preserve its exact custom black lowercase lettering, the dot on the j, the period, and its blue underline, without redesigning anything. Remove only the white background and deliver a genuinely transparent PNG (alpha channel), NOT a drawn checkerboard, no white halo or background. Square composition. Keep the same framing and same colors. This mark will appear in a 28pt navigation header and larger splash screen. No extra elements, no text additions, no shadows.
```

## Just Doodle Wordmark

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final transparent horizontal wordmark for a native drawing app.
Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match the confident bold black felt-tip hand-lettering of its jd. monogram.
Exact text: "Just Doodle." on ONE horizontal line, case-sensitive, with the period at the end only. Draw custom legible friendly signature lettering, slightly imperfect organic contours, substantial rounded black strokes, not thin cursive, not a typeset geometric font. This must read clearly as Just Doodle. The two o letters are open, slightly different handwritten loops.
Composition: very wide 3:1 transparent canvas, lettering spans 90 percent width, centered vertically and horizontally, tight top and bottom margins without clipping any strokes. Black #151515 only, no underline (the app animates an underline separately). Genuine alpha transparency, no white rectangle, no checkerboard. No symbols, doodles, gradients, shadows, borders, notebook rules, mockups, or other text. One finished logo, not a brand sheet.
```

Use case: logo-brand. Final flat, crisp, beautiful hand-lettered logo for The Doodler's Club, a playful independent drawing club. One logo only, no mockup.

STRICT: flat colors only, sharp clean edges, absolutely NO shadow, no glow, no blur, no haze, no gradients, no texture, no dimensional lighting, no neon, no other objects, no decorative marks, no border. Solid opaque white background everywhere except the lettering and blue underline.
